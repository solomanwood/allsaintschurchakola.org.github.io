About All Saints Church

Our History & Mission

All Saints Church, Church of North India (CNI), has been a spiritual beacon in Akola for over 150 years. Our mission is to make disciples of Jesus Christ and equip them to serve in their communities.

We believe in the power of God's Word and the importance of community. Through our various ministries and outreach programs, we strive to provide spiritual guidance, emotional support, and practical help to all who come through our doors.

Our church is committed to being an inclusive, welcoming community where all people—regardless of background or belief—feel valued and accepted.
