About Our Church

The Church serves as a sacred place of worship, prayer, fellowship, and spiritual renewal. It is dedicated to guiding people in faith, bringing them closer to God, and building a strong community rooted in love and compassion.

We welcome you to explore our church, attend our services, and become part of our growing family in Christ. This website provides helpful information about service timings, events, and prayer requests so you can stay connected with us anytime.

As the heart of our parish, All Saints Church is home to many families who share a deep faith and commitment to God. Every year, visitors from nearby places and different regions come to pray, worship, and find peace in the presence of the Lord.

We conduct regular Mass services, prayer meetings, and special celebrations throughout the year, including Holy Days and festivals. The church also offers opportunities for confession, spiritual guidance, and community service.

Throughout the year, we host:

1.Holy Mass Services
2.Prayer & Devotion Meetings
3.Youth & Family Programs
4.Special Feast Day Celebrations
5.Community Worship Events

We invite you to check our schedule and join us in worship as we grow together in faith and love.
