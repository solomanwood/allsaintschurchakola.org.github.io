Our Story

A Living Heritage
of Faith & Grace

Founded in 1873 during the British colonial era, All Saints Church stands as one of the oldest and most beloved churches in the Vidarbha region of Maharashtra. For over 151 years, our congregation has been a sanctuary of worship, community, and spiritual nourishment for the people of Akola.

Affiliated with the Church of North India (CNI) under the Diocese of Amravati, we proudly carry the legacy of colonial Gothic architecture while nurturing a vibrant, modern congregation rooted in ancient faith. Our doors are open to all who seek the peace and love of Christ.

Our Ministries

Every member of our congregation is a minister. We offer a range of ministries and programs through which you can serve, grow in faith, and build lasting community.

Youth Ministry

Nurturing the next generation of faith leaders through Bible study, retreats, and community service.

Women's Fellowship

Empowering women through prayer groups, Bible study, and outreach programs across Akola.

Children's Church

A safe, joyful space for children to discover God's love through stories, crafts, and songs.

Choir & Music

Our choir brings worship to life through hymns, contemporary praise, and traditional liturgical music.

Men's Brotherhood

Fellowship, accountability and service for men of all ages — growing together in Christ.

Outreach & Charity

Serving the underprivileged of Akola through food drives, education support, and medical camps.

Bible Study

Weekly small groups for deep study of Scripture, prayer, and spiritual formation for all ages.

Prayer Ministry

Interceding for our congregation, city, and nation — believing in the power of united prayer.

A Word From Our Pastor
"We are not just a church — we are a family, rooted in Christ, open to all, and sent to serve."

Welcome to All Saints Church, Akola. For over 151 years, this historic congregation has been a beacon of light in the heart of the Vidarbha region. Whether you are a lifelong believer or simply searching for something more, you are welcome here. Our doors, our hearts, and our community are open to you.

We believe in a Gospel that transforms lives, families, and communities. I invite you to join us on Sunday as we worship together, learn together, and grow together in faith and love.
